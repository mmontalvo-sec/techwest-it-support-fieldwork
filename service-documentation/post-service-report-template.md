# Post-Service Report Template

Provided to the client at the conclusion of every engagement. Serves as the written record of work performed, parts replaced, and recommendations going forward.

---

## Service Summary

- Ticket or job number:
- Date completed:
- Technician:
- Device: (laptop / desktop / other; manufacturer and model)

---

## Reported Issue

A short restatement of the issue as the client described it during intake.

---

## Diagnostic Findings

What was found during testing, in plain language. Avoid speculation; report only what was confirmed.

---

## Work Performed

A list of every action taken. This should be detailed enough that a future technician can understand exactly what was done, but written in language the client can also follow.

Example structure:

| Step | Action | Result |
|------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Ran SFC and DISM to repair Windows system files | Reported issues, repaired successfully |
| 2 | Reviewed Event Viewer for recurring errors | Identified service causing repeated entries; disabled |
| 3 | Cleaned startup applications | Disabled 12 unnecessary entries |
| 4 | Applied pending Windows updates | All updates installed; system stable after reboot |
| 5 | Validated client's main applications launch correctly | Confirmed |

---

## Parts Replaced (if any)

| Part | Reason for Replacement | Warranty |
|------|------------------------|----------|
| Example: 500GB SATA SSD | Original HDD slow; reduced boot and app load times | Per manufacturer |

---

## Data Status

- Was client data preserved through the work: (yes / no / partial)
- Backup taken before destructive work: (yes / no / not applicable)
- Backup retained for: (duration)

---

## Validation Performed

- Reboot completed successfully
- Client's main applications tested
- Internet connectivity confirmed
- Printer functionality confirmed (if applicable)
- Other:

---

## Outcome

A short statement of whether the original reported issue is resolved.

---

## Recommendations

Practical next steps for the client. This is one of the most valuable parts of the report.

Examples:
- Schedule a maintenance pass every 12 months
- Implement a backup plan covering the Documents and Pictures folders
- Consider an SSD upgrade in the next year if performance becomes a concern again
- Replace the device within the next several years; the hardware is approaching end of useful life
