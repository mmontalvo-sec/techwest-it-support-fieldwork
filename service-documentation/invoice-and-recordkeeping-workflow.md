# Invoice and Recordkeeping Workflow

A short description of how billing and records are handled at Tech West PC Repair. The goal is consistency: every job follows the same path from intake to final record retention.

---

## Stage 1: Intake

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Client describes issue | Client intake form |
| Diagnostic work authorized | Client intake form, with signature or recorded confirmation |
| Initial cost estimate provided | Client intake form |
| Maximum cost without further authorization agreed | Client intake form |

The intake form is the foundation of the eventual record. No work proceeds without it.

---

## Stage 2: Diagnostic Work

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Symptoms confirmed | Diagnostic checklist |
| Tools and tests run | Diagnostic checklist |
| Findings recorded | Diagnostic checklist |
| Decision point reached | Diagnostic checklist |

If the diagnostic identifies that the cost will exceed the agreed maximum, the client is contacted before any further work proceeds.

---

## Stage 3: Authorized Repair

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Repair authorization received | Note added to intake record |
| Parts ordered or used | Parts list with cost basis |
| Repair performed | Service report draft |
| Validation steps run | Service report draft |

Parts cost is recorded at the time the part is used, not at the time of invoicing, so the record matches what was actually consumed.

---

## Stage 4: Service Report

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Work performed listed | Final service report |
| Parts replaced listed | Final service report |
| Validation outcomes recorded | Final service report |
| Recommendations written | Final service report |

The service report is the primary deliverable to the client beyond the working device itself.

---

## Stage 5: Invoicing

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Labor hours calculated | Invoice |
| Parts cost itemized | Invoice |
| Tax and total calculated | Invoice |
| Payment method noted | Invoice |
| Payment status recorded | Invoice |

The Tech West PC Repair quotation template is used as the basis for both quotes and final invoices to maintain visual consistency across all client documents.

---

## Stage 6: Client Handoff

| Action | Documented In |
|--------|---------------|
| Device returned to client | Pickup record |
| Working state demonstrated | Service report acknowledgment |
| Recommendations communicated verbally | Service report acknowledgment |
| Service report and invoice delivered | Client record |
| Pickup signature obtained (where applicable) | Pickup record |

---

## Stage 7: Record Retention

Each completed engagement produces a client record set:

| Document | Retention |
|----------|-----------|
| Intake form | Retained per business records policy |
| Diagnostic notes | Retained per business records policy |
| Service report | Retained per business records policy |
| Invoice | Retained per local tax and business records requirements |
| Photographs of damage or pre-service state (if any) | Retained until completion confirmed, then archived |

Records do not include client passwords, recovery keys, or any other credential material. Credentials are never persisted beyond the duration of the engagement, and the client is reminded to change any credential that was disclosed during service.

---

## Privacy Note

All client records are stored consistent with the privacy practices described in [privacy-and-sanitization-policy.md](../privacy-and-sanitization-policy.md). Records used in any public-facing case study are sanitized before publication.
