# Portfolio Summary

## Snapshot

Approximately three years of customer-facing IT support and PC repair experience delivered through Tech West PC Repair, a hands-on IT support business operating in Puerto Rico. This portfolio documents that experience in a sanitized, recruiter-friendly format.

---

## What I Do

I diagnose, document, repair, optimize, restore, and explain technical issues for real users. The work spans Windows endpoint support, hardware diagnostics and replacement, printer troubleshooting, backup planning, malware and ransomware impact assessment, Active Directory user account management, DNS and DHCP troubleshooting, laser printer consumable management, and post-service client education.

Every job follows a repeatable workflow: intake, diagnostic questioning, inspection, testing, documentation, client communication, approved repair, validation, and follow-up recommendations.

## What Recruiters Get From This Portfolio

A clear demonstration that the candidate can perform real IT support work, not just describe it. Each case study walks through the actual diagnostic and repair process, the tools used, the outcome, and the recommendations provided to the client.

The portfolio also demonstrates communication skills that are difficult to verify from a resume alone: explaining technical concepts to non-technical users, managing expectations when outcomes are uncertain, and producing documentation that other technicians or clients can follow.
